I

Reading the plan

Five plates on how a plan is read. Start with the plan itself, then see how the same selection travels through every view.

01The plan

Every step, and what each one reads

Does
Draws the plan as steps in columns. A step sits one column to the right of every step it reads.
Means
Read from left to right to follow the work. Steps stacked in one column can run side by side.

Select a step to open its details. The plan stays where it is.

02The thread

The selected step is the same step everywhere

Does
Marks the selected step with a red edge, in every view.
Means
The selection belongs to the plan, not to one view. Choose a step in any plate on this page and every other plate selects it too.

The strip is the plan in reading order. Its mark follows the step above.

03Four distances

Four ways to look at the same plan

Does
Shows the plan as a shape, as a sequence, as a list, or one step at a time.
Means
Choose the distance that answers your question. The selection comes with you when you change it.

Canvas

The shape of the work, and what depends on what.

Strip

The order of the work, one step after another.

List

Every step as a row, for scanning and filtering.

Details

One step, with what it reads and what reads it.

04Overview

A map of the whole plan

Does
Shows the whole canvas in a small box. The red frame is the part you can see now.
Means
Use it to find your place in a long plan without losing it.
05Across and down

Two directions, two meanings

Does
Across shows what a step reads, and what reads it. Down shows steps that can run alongside, in plan order.
Means
A step waits for the steps it reads. Steps in one column do not wait for each other.
06Restriction

A filter says what it hides

Does
Names the filter, the count it shows, and what it hides. One control clears it.
Means
A step you ask for is never hidden without a reason. Choosing a hidden step clears the filter and says so.

II

Words before colour

A state is a word, a dot and a tone, in that order of importance. Each state says what it means and what to do next.

07The ten states

Each state says what it means, and what you do next

Does
Shows the state of every step. The word comes first, the dot second, and the colour third.
Means
A plan read in greyscale says the same things. Nothing depends on colour alone.
08Money

Every number says what it is

Does
Says whether a figure was recorded, estimated, not known, or not a model cost.
Means
An estimate is never shown as a bill. A figure that is not known is never shown as zero.
09Colour

Colour is the third signal

Does
The word and the dot carry the state. The colour adds a tone, and nothing depends on it alone.
Means
Turn on Colour off at the top of the page. Every state still reads.

    IV

    Motion, touch and notices

    Motion explains a change. A phone gets the same plan in a shape that fits, and a notice says what it needs from you.

    13Motion

    Only what explains a change moves

    Does
    Moves to show a change. The line into a running step moves. Nothing else on the page does.
    Means
    With reduced motion on, the line stops and colours still change at once.
    Running

    Measured under reduced motion: no animation loops.

    14Redraw

    A redraw keeps every place

    Does
    Turns each step into a block in the same place, then fills the blocks again.
    Means
    Nothing jumps while the plan reloads, so you can keep your place in it.

    Measured: layout shift on redraw is 0.

    15Notices

    A passing note leaves; a failure stays

    Does
    A saved note leaves by itself after four seconds. A failure stays until you close it.
    Means
    Nothing important leaves on a timer. Read a failure before it goes.
    16Phones

    Under 360 pixels, the canvas becomes the list

    Does
    Draws the same steps, in the same order, as a list when the space is narrow.
    Means
    Nothing is shrunk to fit. Each step keeps its words and its cost.
    17Keyboard

    The plan works without a mouse

    Does
    Tab walks the steps in reading order, column by column. Enter opens the details. Escape closes them and returns focus to the step.
    Means
    Someone who cannot use a pointer reads the plan in the same order as everyone else.
    • Tab steps in reading order
    • Enter opens a step's details
    • Esc closes them and returns to the step
    • + − 0 zoom, then reset, with the canvas focused
    • Arrow keys scroll the canvas when it has focus

    Red marks what is happening now

    22Measured

    What the browser capture measured

    Does
    Runs each claim on this page in headless Chromium and records the number.
    Means
    A claim that fails stops the capture with a non-zero exit. Nothing on this page is a claim the capture does not check.
    Theme
    Follows the system, and an explicit choice overrides it
    Fonts
    Archivo, Instrument Sans and JetBrains Mono resolve
    Titles
    36 px in the wide column, 28 px in the narrow one; the masthead steps with its width
    Brief
    Opening the assistant narrows the brief's column, and the plan does not move
    Details
    A step does not move when the panel opens, stays clear of it, and takes focus
    Thread
    A step chosen in one plate is selected in every plate
    Overview
    The map sits above the canvas, and a click on it moves the canvas
    Keyboard
    The skip link is first; Tab walks eight steps in reading order; arrow keys scroll the plan
    Restriction
    Shows the count in words, and a hidden step clears the filter
    Redraw
    Layout shift 0
    Motion
    No looping animation under reduced motion
    Phone
    No sideways scroll at 390 pixels; the plan is drawn as its list

    Ten distinct state words, each with a meaning and a next step

    23Limits

    What was not checked

    Does
    Lists the boundary of this evidence.
    Means
    Read these before trusting the design in a real product.
    • Chromium only. Safari and Firefox were not run.
    • No screen reader was run, and no real phone was used.
    • All data is sample data. No provider was called.
    • Home, Library, Insights and Settings are described in the plan, not built.
    • The full test suite and the full build were not run for this page.
